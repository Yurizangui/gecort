Terms of Service

1. What GECORT is

GECORT is a private tool that turns podcast episodes into video clips (long horizontal videos and short vertical videos) and publishes them to the operator’s own accounts, the channel “Bungle Bangle da Banda”, on YouTube and TikTok. It is not offered to the public: only the operator uses it, and it only posts to the operator’s own accounts.

2. Content

  • Clips are made only from podcasts whose creators have authorized publication. Every video credits the original source.
  • If you own content and want a clip taken down, contact us (see “Contact”) and it will be removed.
  • Published clips follow the TikTok and YouTube Community Guidelines and Terms of Service.

3. Platform use

GECORT uses only the official APIs (TikTok Content Posting API and YouTube Data API), with authorization granted by the operator, who can revoke it at any time in their account settings.

4. No warranty

The tool is provided “as is”, without any guarantee of uninterrupted operation. The operator is responsible for the content they publish.

5. Changes

These terms may be updated. The date of the last change is shown at the top of this page.
